Trainloop is an Android training log that brings two modes together in one app: Intervals and Strength. It works without an account and without a connection: your data stays on your phone.

INTERVALS
• Ready-to-use timers: Tabata 20/10, HIIT 40/20, HIIT 30/30, EMOM 10 min and AMRAP 12 min.
• Build your own timers with repeatable blocks.
• Keeps counting with the screen off or another app open: countdown notification, beeps, voice (if your phone has a voice installed for your language) and vibration at every phase change.
• If Android closes the app mid-session, you can resume it, save it as incomplete or discard it when you come back.

STRENGTH
• Strength routines with sets, reps, weight and rest.
• Library of 105 exercises in English, Spanish and Portuguese, plus your own.
• Every set shows your last result. "Same as before" repeats your last set in one tap.
• Rest starts automatically when you check off a set and alerts you when it ends.
• Kilograms or pounds, optional RPE and set types (warm-up, to failure, drop set).

PROGRESS
• Session history, week view, calendar and personal records.

YOUR DATA
• No account, no sign-up.
• Backup and restore to a file you keep wherever you want, plus CSV export.
• Local reminders on the days and time you choose.

ADS
Trainloop is free with ads. Ads never appear during a session. You can remove them with a one-time purchase.

Available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
